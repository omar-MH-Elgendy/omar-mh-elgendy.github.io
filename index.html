Insights report

Where marketplace profit leaks

Returns and failed deliveries took back almost a third of the profit earned on good orders. Jumia returns 29% of gross sales, Noon charges 25% in fees, and the monthly margin fell from 29% to 8%.

31%of profit lost
18,109EGP lost on 170 lines
Decision: pull repeat-return products on Jumia, price Noon items with the fee included, review prices on fast-growing products.

Insights report

Returns and failed deliveries

A failed delivery costs about 132 EGP per order on Jumia and about 15 on Noon. One product had problems on 9 of 13 orders and made no profit. Money waiting on delivery was small and on time.

Decision: confirm customer details before shipping on Jumia, claim every late return, pause the product that loses on most sales.

Partner reporting with row-level security

Case study

Redesign of an operations reporting portal

The portal had 25 pages, five different figures for the same balance and admin tools mixed in with partner pages. I audited every page, wrote the specification for each partner view and built an interactive prototype with a date-range calendar, drill-down tables and CSV export, using sample data.
