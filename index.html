Internal tool

Control Center: an approvals app for daily operations

Everything that needs my decision arrives as a card: the issue, the evidence, a free-text box for my reasoning and one button to execute. It works on phone and PC, and a dispatcher I built reads the approved decisions, helps carry them out with my coding assistant, and sends me any new decisions it runs into.

Where marketplace profit leaks

Returns and failed deliveries took back 18% of the profit earned on good orders. Jumia keeps under 8% of its sales as profit against 22% on Noon, and Noon charges 25% in fees.

Insights report

Returns and failed deliveries

A failed delivery costs about 92 EGP per order on Jumia and 71 on Noon, and a return about 94 and 80. One high-volume product had problems on 9 of 35 orders and lost money overall. 128 orders worth 47,099 EGP are still in progress.

Decision: confirm customer details before shipping, claim every return, raise the price of or pause the product that loses money.
